Built for flow

Your files, with a sense of direction.

Personal Navigator keeps the first view calm, then lets you dig deeper when you need to.

Search the way you remember

Use a project name, an approximate spelling, or a related idea. Results are scored locally and grouped into useful roots instead of a wall of files.

bionic handECHOrelated project

A map, not a list

See the relationship between Personal, its main areas, and the project you want.

Detail on demand

Start with project roots. Switch to detailed mode for matching subfolders and individual files.

Private by construction

The index stays on your computer. There is no account, cloud database, telemetry service, or search API between you and your files.
